About

Artist-led visual production.

I’m Joshua Pearman, a UK-based creative working professionally as BVDLVD. Since 2018, I’ve built a career across music, video production, live shows and artist development, combining the instincts of an artist with the practical skillset of a production lead.

My work spans music videos, release campaigns, multicam live edits, studio production, tour logistics and visual direction. Whether I’m behind the camera, in the edit, managing a live setup or shaping an album world, the goal is the same: make the project feel clear, atmospheric and impossible to ignore.
